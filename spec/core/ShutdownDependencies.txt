ShutdownDependencies maps the fallible shutdown ordering to a bounded dependency
chain: dependent plugin -> plugin -> resident services -> database, and provider
-> resident services. The positive model stops at the failed child and retries
that stage. The unsafe variant continues, matching the old ignored-error paths.

Implementation:
- PluginHost._shutdown_owned withdraws management admission, closes its jobs,
  and detaches generations in reverse topological order. Any failed generation
  stops the traversal, preserves dependencies, and populates shutdown_errors.
- StubRuntimeHandle.stop_async checks plugin and provider errors before moving
  to resident services. Resident hook failures propagate. Successful hooks are
  cleared so retry does not invoke them twice. Database close is last.
- Plugin/provider handles retain failed work as described in the publication
  and ProviderCleanupOwnership models and their boundary tests.

This model assumes serialized shutdown and quiescent public admission. It is a
safety abstraction with two representative dependent plugins, not a generated
proof of every Python call or a liveness claim about arbitrary shutdown hooks.
